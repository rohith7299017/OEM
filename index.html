FAQ

Common questions.

How does the AI proctoring work?

Our system uses your device camera for continuous facial verification and gaze tracking. It flags suspicious behaviour like looking away, multiple faces, or ambient voices — all reviewed in an audit trail.

What if a student loses internet mid-exam?

Answers are cached locally in an encrypted sandbox and auto-sync the moment connectivity returns. No answers are ever lost.

Does it integrate with our LMS?

Yes — we support LTI 1.3, Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard, Google Classroom, and custom REST/SSO integrations out of the box.

What devices do students need?

Any modern browser on Windows, macOS, ChromeOS, iPads, or Android tablets. A lightweight optional app adds lockdown browser features.

Is my data safe?

All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We are ISO 27001 certified and GDPR / FERPA compliant.
